HuskyLens Flappy Bird

Not connected (Space / click also flaps)

Plug in the ESP32, click Connect ESP32 and pick its COM port. Your ESP32 makes the bird flap by sending Serial.println("F"); at 115200 baud (FLAP, JUMP, UP and 1 also work). Use Chrome or Edge, and close the Arduino Serial Monitor first, or the port will be busy.
